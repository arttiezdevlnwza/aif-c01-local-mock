AIF-C01 OPUS Practice — v8.2 Choice Wording Audit

Goal
- Prevent answer choices from revealing their own meaning in questions that are supposed to test whether the learner can distinguish a service, metric, technique, feature, or named concept.
- Keep detailed reasoning in the post-answer explanation/Summary, not inside the pre-answer choices.

Intentional exceptions
- Some questions explicitly ask which explanation, reason, action, design, or pairing is correct. Those choices must contain descriptive wording because the wording itself is what is being evaluated.
- Examples include prompt-injection mitigations, pairing two services to two requirements, and questions asking what a concept means.
